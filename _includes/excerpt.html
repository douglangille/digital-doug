{%- comment -%} Sets `lead` (a front-matter excerpt, or empty) and `thumb` for include.post. An auto excerpt is the document's first block (the leading H1), so it is dropped when it matches the start of the body text. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ep = include.post -%} {%- assign thumb = ep.image | default: ep.header.teaser -%} {%- assign lead = ep.excerpt | strip_html | strip | normalize_whitespace -%} {%- assign body_text = ep.content | strip_html | strip | normalize_whitespace -%} {%- assign lead_len = lead | size -%} {%- assign body_start = body_text | slice: 0, lead_len -%} {%- if lead_len == 0 or body_start == lead -%}{%- assign lead = "" -%}{%- endif -%}
